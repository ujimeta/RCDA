Community Engagement & Advocacy

Traditional and religious leader mobilization, stakeholder policy dialogues, and grassroots advocacy frameworks.

Past Activities & Milestones

RCDA has built its foundational success on deep community trust, engaging traditional rulers, religious leaders (Imams and pastors), and women leaders as active champions for grassroots health and development programs.

Milestones include establishing high-level consultative forums that successfully resolved local barriers to health interventions, securing community co-ownership across target project areas.

Ongoing Projects & Current Operations

Our current operations anchor all health and development interventions through continuous community dialogues and town hall meetings. We empower local gatekeepers with accurate information to champion health-seeking behaviors.

Teams actively collaborate with local government structures to advocate for sustainable funding and policy support for grassroots primary healthcare programs.
