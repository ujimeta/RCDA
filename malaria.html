Malaria Interventions

Active case finding, Rapid Diagnostic Testing (RDT), treatment, and community distribution networks across Kano grassroots areas.

Past Activities & Milestones

Over previous funding cycles, RCDA successfully established grassroots intervention structures across target local government areas in Kano State. Historical operations focused heavily on building community trust, training local health volunteers, and deploying pilot distribution networks for Insecticide-Treated Nets (ITNs).

Milestones achieved include the training of over 150 Community-Oriented Resource Persons (CORPs) and conducting massive community awareness campaigns that significantly shifted local health-seeking behaviors toward early testing rather than presumptive self-medication.

Ongoing Projects & Current Operations

Malaria remains a significant health burden across rural and peri-urban settlements. Currently, RCDA executes rigorous door-to-door screening protocols designed to close the treatment gap by bringing diagnostic and curative services directly to household doorsteps.

Our teams actively manage uncomplicated malaria cases using Artemisinin-based Combination Therapy (ACT) while simultaneously coordinating targeted net maintenance distributions and continuous community health education.
