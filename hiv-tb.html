HIV/AIDS & TB Integration

Index testing, active antiretroviral therapy (ART) linkages, and C:J-SOFT TB presumptive screening protocols.

Past Activities & Milestones

RCDA has historical experience running community-based sensitization campaigns to demystify HIV and Tuberculosis, combating intense social stigmas that traditionally prevent infected individuals from seeking early treatment.

Milestones include executing targeted community screenings, distributing prevention commodities, and establishing robust referral networks linking vulnerable clusters directly to accredited treatment facilities.

Ongoing Projects & Current Operations

Our ongoing operations integrate HIV index testing with active Community-Based Tuberculosis (C:J-SOFT) screening protocols. Trained field volunteers actively screen community members for persistent coughs, night sweats, and unexplained weight loss.

Suspected cases are referred immediately for GeneXpert testing while HIV-positive clients receive confidential counseling and seamless ART enrollment support.
