What We Do

RCDA operates a comprehensive community-based service delivery model across Kano State, bridging households and formal primary healthcare centers through structured outreach, active case-finding, and robust referral pathways.

Our Core Service Pillars

  • Active Case-Finding & Disease Surveillance: Mobilizing community volunteers for localized screening, testing, and contact tracing for malaria, HIV, TB, and outbreaks like diphtheria.
  • Maternal, Newborn & Child Health (MNCH): Delivering community education on reproductive health, child spacing, routine immunization, and nutritional screenings (MUAC).
  • Community Systems Strengthening: Partnering with local stakeholders, traditional leaders, and civil society networks to build sustainable health-seeking behaviors.
  • Advocacy & Sensitization: Conducting household campaigns to eliminate health stigmas, debunk medical myths, and promote healthcare uptake.

Our Implementation Approach

We work hand-in-hand with local government health authorities, technical partners like SFH and KNCV, and trained Community-Based Volunteers (CORPs) to ensure that life-saving interventions reach the most vulnerable grassroots populations.
