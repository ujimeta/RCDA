Systems Strengthening & Disease Surveillance

Community-based early warning networks, health facility data validation, and epidemic preparedness training.

Past Activities & Milestones

RCDA has a strong background in supporting local health committees and strengthening grassroots data collection structures to improve transparency and tracking within primary healthcare centers.

Milestones include training local surveillance focal persons, conducting data quality assessments, and establishing community-level reporting linkages for prompt epidemic alert escalation.

Ongoing Projects & Current Operations

Our current operations focus on deploying real-time community-based disease surveillance models. Trained community informants monitor and report unusual clustering of symptoms or suspected disease outbreaks directly to municipal health authorities.

We also conduct continuous capacity-building workshops for local health workers on accurate data management, inventory tracking, and reporting compliance.
