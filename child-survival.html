Child Survival Services

Routine immunization tracking, neonatal care advocacy, and community-based preventive health interventions.

Past Activities & Milestones

Historically, RCDA has partnered with state health agencies to mobilize communities during polio immunization campaigns and routine vaccination catch-up drives across hard-to-reach settlements in Kano State.

Milestones include organizing community town halls with local leaders to address vaccine hesitancy, successfully boosting local immunization turnout figures significantly across target wards.

Ongoing Projects & Current Operations

Our current child survival operations focus heavily on tracking defaulters and ensuring mothers complete the full routine immunization schedule for infants under one year.

Field teams conduct household visits to educate parents on basic neonatal hygiene, temperature monitoring, and early recognition of danger signs in newborns.
