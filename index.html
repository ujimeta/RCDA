Malaria Interventions

Active case finding, Rapid Diagnostic Testing (RDT), treatment, and community distribution networks across Kano grassroots areas.

Overview of Activities & Field Operations

Malaria remains a significant health burden across rural and peri-urban settlements in Kano State. RCDA executes rigorous community-directed interventions designed to close the treatment gap by bringing diagnostic and curative services directly to household doorsteps.

Through our network of trained Community-Oriented Resource Persons (CORPs) and volunteers, we conduct door-to-door screenings, manage uncomplicated malaria cases using Artemisinin-based Combination Therapy (ACT), and coordinate strategic Insecticide-Treated Net (ITN) distribution campaigns combined with health education.
