Rahama Community Development Association (RCDA) works at the grassroots intersection of public health, disease case-finding, and sustainable socioeconomic empowerment across Kano State.

A Welcome from the Founder

At Rahama Community Development Association, we believe that lasting change begins with communities themselves. Our work is rooted in compassion, partnership and practical action, connecting people with the health services, knowledge and opportunities they need to thrive. Through collaboration with communities, government institutions, development partners and other stakeholders, RCDA remains committed to strengthening healthier, more resilient and empowered communities across Kano State and beyond.

Haj. Hajara Muhammad Argungu
Founder / Executive Director, RCDA

Our Community-Centered Model

RCDA uses a practical community-centered pathway that connects awareness, identification, testing, referral and follow-up to strengthen health outcomes.
