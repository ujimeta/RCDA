Our Community Case-Finding & Referral Pathway

A standardized 5-step framework ensuring consistent community sensitization, screening, testing, and follow-up.

01. Sensitization

Household & community awareness drives on malaria, HIV, TB, and diphtheria.

02. Screening

Community volunteers & CORPs identify presumptive and at-risk cases at the household level.

03. Testing / Tracing

Malaria RDT, HIV testing, TB presumptive screening, and diphtheria contact listing.

04. Referral Linkage

Confirmed cases linked directly to health facilities for treatment, ART, or preventive therapy.

05. Follow-up & M&E

Treatment adherence support, data capture, and reporting to LGA/State surveillance.
