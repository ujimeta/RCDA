About Rahama Community Development Association

Rahama Community Development Association (RCDA) is a registered, non-discriminatory, not-for-profit community-based organization (CBO) founded in 2014 and formally registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC/IT/NO. 92853) in 2016.

Our work is anchored on the belief that lasting change in community health outcomes is achieved when services are taken closer to households through trained community volunteers, Community-Oriented Resource Persons (CORPs), and structured referral linkages to the formal health system.

Our Eight Thematic Focus Areas

Delivered through a community-centered service model linking households directly to primary healthcare systems.

Reproductive Health & Child Spacing

Community-based provision of family planning information, dispelling myths, and reducing barriers to maternal care.

Nutrition Services

MUAC screening, growth monitoring, maternal nutrition counseling, and timely referral of malnourished children.

Child Survival Services

Promoting routine immunization uptake and managing common childhood illnesses via trained volunteers.

Malaria Interventions

Rapid Diagnostic Testing (RDT), Insecticide-Treated Net (ITN) distribution campaigns, and demand creation.

HIV/AIDS Case Finding

Community mobilization, testing support, index testing, and active linkage to ART care facilities.

TB Case Finding

Partnering on the TB C:J-SOFT project for contact tracing, presumptive screening, and preventive therapy follow-up.

Diphtheria Contact Tracing

Surveillance, suspect identification, close contact tracing, and IDSR reporting in line with national protocols.

Community Systems Strengthening

OVC support, multi-sectoral advocacy, and vocational skills initiatives for sustainable communities.

Our Community Case-Finding & Referral Pathway

A standardized 5-step framework ensuring consistent community sensitization, screening, testing, and follow-up.

01. Sensitization

Household & community awareness drives on malaria, HIV, TB, and diphtheria.

02. Screening

Community volunteers & CORPs identify presumptive and at-risk cases at the household level.

03. Testing / Tracing

Malaria RDT, HIV testing, TB presumptive screening, and diphtheria contact listing.

04. Referral Linkage

Confirmed cases linked directly to health facilities for treatment, ART, or preventive therapy.

05. Follow-up & M&E

Treatment adherence support, data capture, and reporting to LGA/State surveillance.
