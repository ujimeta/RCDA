A MESSAGE FROM OUR FOUNDER

Welcome to Rahama Community Development Association

It is my pleasure to welcome you to Rahama Community Development Association (RCDA). Since our establishment, we have remained committed to working alongside communities to address health and development challenges through practical, inclusive and community-centred solutions. We believe that sustainable change begins when communities are empowered to participate meaningfully in identifying their needs, accessing essential services and shaping solutions that improve their wellbeing. Through partnerships, community engagement and locally driven action, RCDA continues to work toward healthier, stronger and more resilient communities across Kano State and beyond.

Haj. Hajara Muhammad Argungu
Founder & Executive Director

Our Eight Thematic Focus Areas

Reproductive Health & Child Spacing

Community-based provision of family planning information, dispelling myths, and reducing barriers to maternal care.

Nutrition Services

MUAC screening, growth monitoring, maternal nutrition counseling, and timely referral of malnourished children.

Child Survival Services

Promoting routine immunization uptake and managing common childhood illnesses via trained volunteers.

🦟

Malaria Interventions

Rapid Diagnostic Testing (RDT), Insecticide-Treated Net (ITN) distribution campaigns, and demand creation.

View Activities →
🎗️

HIV/AIDS Case Finding

Community mobilization, testing support, index testing, and active linkage to ART care facilities.

View Activities →
🫁

TB Case Finding

Partnering on the TB C:J-SOFT project for contact tracing, presumptive screening, and preventive therapy follow-up.

Diphtheria Contact Tracing

Surveillance, suspect identification, close contact tracing, and IDSR reporting in line with national protocols.
