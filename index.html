About RCDA Nigeria

Rahama Community Development Association (RCDA) is a leading grassroots organization dedicated to improving public health, maternal and child survival, and socio-economic wellbeing across Kano State and neighboring regions.

Through close partnerships with community leaders, healthcare workers, and international stakeholders, we execute targeted, high-impact interventions designed to reach the most vulnerable populations.
