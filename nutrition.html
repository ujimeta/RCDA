Nutrition Services

MUAC screenings, maternal infant counseling, and community-based management of acute malnutrition across Kano State.

Past Activities & Milestones

RCDA has historically spearheaded community nutrition education initiatives, focusing heavily on infant and young child feeding (IYCF) practices. Past operations involved conducting grassroots cooking demonstrations using locally available, nutrient-dense foods and distributing micronutrient powders to vulnerable households.

Milestones include screening thousands of children under five and establishing active referral pathways linking remote communities with primary health center stabilization units.

Ongoing Projects & Current Operations

Given the pressing regional stunting context, our current field operations prioritize active Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) screenings conducted by trained community volunteers. Children identified with Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM) or Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM) receive immediate therapeutic support and direct linkages to care.

We also run ongoing mother support groups that provide continuous counseling on exclusive breastfeeding and complementary dietary diversity.
