How We Work

Our Community-Centred Model

A practical pathway connecting communities to identification, testing, referral, follow-up and continuous learning to improve health and development outcomes.

From Community Engagement to Lasting Impact

RCDA works at the community level to identify needs, connect people with appropriate services and strengthen the systems that support healthier communities. Our model brings these activities together as a connected pathway, allowing community engagement, service delivery, referral, follow-up and learning to reinforce one another.
