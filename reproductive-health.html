Reproductive Health & Spacing

Maternal mortality reduction, family planning education, and safe motherhood advocacy across Kano communities.

Past Activities & Milestones

RCDA has historical roots in community-level maternal health advocacy. Past interventions centered on breaking cultural barriers around family health, facilitating safe delivery referrals, and training traditional birth attendants on timely emergency escalation protocols.

Milestones include reaching thousands of women of reproductive age with structured health talks and distributing vital informational materials dispelling common health myths.

Ongoing Projects & Current Operations

Our current operations focus on expanding access to accurate family planning information and counseling services in close collaboration with primary health care centers. We conduct community dialogues involving community and religious leaders to build consensus around maternal health and birth spacing.

Teams work closely with pregnant women to encourage early Antenatal Care (ANC) attendance and institutional deliveries.
