Water, Sanitation & Hygiene (WASH)

Safe water access, community-led total sanitation (CLTS), and hygiene promotion campaigns across rural settlements.

.

Past Activities & Milestones

RCDA has a rich history of executing community-level WASH interventions, focusing heavily on rural water point rehabilitations and promoting safe hygiene practices to prevent waterborne disease outbreaks.

Milestones include facilitating community-led total sanitation (CLTS) triggers, constructing school sanitation blocks, and training village water committees on sustainable facility management.

Ongoing Projects & Current Operations

Our current WASH operations prioritize safe water quality testing, household water treatment education, and aggressive handwashing promotion campaigns in rural schools and public market squares.

Teams work hand-in-hand with community leaders to eliminate open defecation and ensure sustained maintenance of community water assets.
