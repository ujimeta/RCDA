Who We Serve

RCDA focuses its interventions primarily on marginalized and underserved grassroots populations across Kano State, with a strong emphasis on communities facing barriers to healthcare access.

Target Demographics

  • Women of Childbearing Age: Providing critical maternal education, family planning support, and antenatal care sensitization.
  • Children Under Five: Conducting routine nutrition screenings (MUAC), immunization tracking, and malaria/child survival case management.
  • Rural & Hard-to-Reach Communities: Reaching populations in remote Local Government Areas with mobile outreach, disease surveillance, and health promotion.
  • Vulnerable Households: Empowering low-income families with health literacy and referral pathways to primary healthcare centers.

Geographic Reach

Our operations span multiple Local Government Areas (LGAs) within Kano State, working directly with local health committees, community leaders, and primary healthcare facilities to ensure maximum local impact.
