Who We Are

Rahama Community Development Association (RCDA) is a grassroots non-governmental organization founded in 2014 and officially registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) in Nigeria in 2016 (CAC/IT/NO. 92853).

Our Vision

A healthy, empowered, and self-reliant community where vulnerable populations have equitable access to quality healthcare, nutrition, and socioeconomic opportunities.

Our Mission

To bridge the gap between primary healthcare systems and underserved grassroots communities in Kano State through active case-finding, community sensitization, robust disease surveillance, and collaborative empowerment partnerships.

Leadership & Governance

RCDA is led by experienced public health advocates and community development specialists, headed by our Executive Director, Haj. Hajara Muhammad Argungu, driving grassroots interventions across multiple Local Government Areas in Kano State.
